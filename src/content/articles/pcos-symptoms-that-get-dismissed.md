PCOS is common, but diagnosis is often delayed for years, partly because
its symptoms tend to be addressed one at a time rather than considered
together.

## Symptoms worth connecting, not treating separately

Irregular cycles, acne that doesn't respond to typical treatment, and
unexplained changes in hair growth or weight can each get dismissed
individually. Together, they're a pattern worth raising with a doctor as a
set, not one at a time.

"Rest day" often gets interpreted as doing nothing at all, but the
research on recovery points to light activity being just as useful, and
sometimes more useful, than total inactivity.

## What active recovery actually does

Light movement — an easy walk, gentle mobility work — keeps blood flow
elevated to recovering muscle without adding meaningful fatigue. That can
speed up how ready a muscle feels for the next hard session compared to a
fully sedentary day.

The exception is genuine overtraining or illness, where full rest is the
right call. For routine recovery between normal training days, "easy
movement" tends to beat "no movement" for how you feel the next day.

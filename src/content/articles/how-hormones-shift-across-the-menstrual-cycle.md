Energy, appetite, and mood shifting across the month isn't random — it
roughly tracks estrogen and progesterone, which rise and fall in a
predictable pattern across a cycle.

## The general shape

Estrogen rises through the first half of the cycle, peaking around ovulation.
Progesterone rises afterward, through the second half, then both drop before
a period starts — a drop that's linked to PMS-type symptoms for many people.

Knowing the general shape can make month-to-month shifts feel less
unpredictable, even without tracking exact hormone levels.

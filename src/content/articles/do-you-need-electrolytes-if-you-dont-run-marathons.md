Electrolyte products are usually marketed around endurance sport, which
makes it easy to assume they're irrelevant if your workouts are shorter or
less intense. That's mostly, but not entirely, true.

## When it actually matters

For most moderate exercise under an hour, plain water and normal food intake
replace what's lost through sweat. Electrolytes become more relevant with
long duration training, heavy sweating in heat, or a diet that's
already low in sodium.

Outside of those situations, an electrolyte drink is mostly optional —
useful for taste and habit, not something most people are physiologically
short on.

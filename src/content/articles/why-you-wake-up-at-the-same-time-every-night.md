Waking at a near-identical time most nights, even without an alarm, is
common enough to have a few well-understood explanations.

## Common causes

Light exposure through curtains at a consistent hour, a bladder cycle tied
to evening fluid intake, and the natural dip in sleep depth that happens in
the second half of the night are the most frequent culprits.

If it's consistently disruptive rather than a brief, easy return to sleep,
it's worth mentioning to a doctor rather than assuming it's unfixable.

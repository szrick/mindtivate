Whether a nap helps or hurts nighttime sleep comes down mostly to when
and how long you nap, not whether napping itself is a good or bad habit.

## What tends to work

A short nap (20–30 minutes) earlier in the afternoon is generally low-risk
for nighttime sleep. Longer naps, or ones taken late in the day, are more
likely to reduce the pressure to fall asleep at your normal bedtime.

If nighttime sleep is already a struggle, cutting naps entirely for a
couple of weeks is a reasonable way to test whether they're a contributing
factor.

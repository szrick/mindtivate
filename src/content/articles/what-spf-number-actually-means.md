SPF measures protection against UVB rays specifically, and the scale isn't
linear — SPF 30 blocks about 97% of UVB, SPF 50 about 98%. The jump from 30
to 50 is smaller in practice than the numbers suggest.

## What matters more than the number

Reapplication and amount applied make a bigger real-world difference than
chasing a higher SPF number. A high-SPF product applied too thinly, or not
reapplied after sweating or swimming, offers less protection than the label
implies.

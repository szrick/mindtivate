Eye creams are often formulated with the same core actives as a
face moisturizer, just in a smaller, pricier jar. That doesn't make them
useless — the skin around the eyes is thinner and can tolerate less active
ingredients — but it does mean a dedicated product isn't strictly required.

## When a separate product makes sense

If your face moisturizer contains a strong active (a high-percentage retinoid
or acid) that irritates the eye area, a gentler dedicated eye product can be
worth it. Otherwise, a fragrance-free facial moisturizer applied carefully
usually does the same job.

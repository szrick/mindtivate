There's no universal correct hair-washing frequency — it depends mostly on
how much oil your scalp naturally produces, plus hair texture and styling
habits.

## A rough starting point

Finer, straighter hair tends to show oil faster and often needs washing
every 1–2 days. Coarser or curlier hair holds moisture differently and can
often go 3–7 days between washes without looking or feeling unwashed.

Stretching the interval gradually, rather than jumping straight from daily
to weekly, tends to be the more comfortable way to find your own number.

Core body temperature naturally drops as part of falling asleep, and a
room that's too warm can work against that process.

## The commonly cited range

Around 65°F (18°C) is frequently cited as ideal, though the useful range for
most people is closer to 60–67°F. The right number varies by person, but
"slightly cooler than feels cozy while awake" is a reasonable rule of
thumb, since comfort while awake and comfort while falling asleep aren't
quite the same thing.

Attachment style — the patterns learned early in life about closeness and
security — tends to become more visible during stress, not during calm,
easy stretches of a relationship.

## Why stress is when it surfaces

Under low stress, most people can regulate their reactions regardless of
attachment style. Under real pressure, the earlier learned pattern — seeking
reassurance, withdrawing, or something in between — tends to take over more
automatically.

Recognizing that pattern for what it is, rather than reading too much into
one stressful week, can prevent an isolated hard patch from being treated
as a verdict on the whole relationship.

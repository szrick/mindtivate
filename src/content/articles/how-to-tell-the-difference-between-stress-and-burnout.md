Stress and burnout share symptoms, but they're not the same thing, and
treating burnout like ordinary stress — pushing through, resting on the
weekend — often doesn't help.

## A rough distinction

Stress tends to come with a sense that things are manageable, just
demanding. Burnout tends to come with emotional exhaustion, cynicism, and a
drop in the sense that effort leads anywhere.

If rest during downtime isn't restoring your energy the way it used to,
that's a signal worth taking seriously rather than pushing past.

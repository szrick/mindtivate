Willpower is a limited, fluctuating resource — it's lower when you're
tired, stressed, or hungry, which is exactly when a new habit is most likely
to get skipped.

## What holds up better than willpower

Habit research points to environment design — reducing the friction to do
the thing you want, and increasing the friction to do the thing you don't —
as more durable than relying on motivation in the moment.

That's a big part of why "just be more disciplined" advice tends not to
stick: it asks a limited resource to do a job that environment design does
more reliably.

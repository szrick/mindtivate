"Drink more water for better skin" is repeated so often it's treated as
settled science. The actual research is thinner than the advice suggests.

## What the evidence actually shows

Severe dehydration does affect skin, but for people who are already
adequately hydrated, drinking noticeably more water hasn't been shown to
meaningfully change skin appearance in most studies. Topical moisture
(what's applied to the skin) tends to matter more for how skin looks than
how much water someone drinks.

Staying hydrated is still worth doing for overall health — it's just not a
strong lever for skin specifically once you're already drinking enough.

Training that worked without much thought in your 20s can start to feel
less forgiving in your 30s — not because your body is suddenly fragile, but
because recovery capacity and joint tolerance shift gradually.

## What tends to matter more

Prioritizing consistent strength training (to offset the gradual muscle loss
that starts in this decade), and building in more deliberate recovery
between hard sessions, tend to pay off more than they did a decade earlier.

None of this means slowing down — it means being a bit more deliberate about
recovery than instinct alone might suggest.

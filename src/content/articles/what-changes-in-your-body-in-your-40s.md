Some changes in your 40s are well-documented — a gradual decline in
muscle mass without resistance training, and hormonal shifts as
perimenopause approaches for many women. Others get blamed on age more than
the evidence supports.

## What's actually driven by age vs. habit

Metabolic slowdown is real but modest — much of the "40s metabolism" effect
traces back to gradual muscle loss and reduced activity, both of which
respond to training, not just to turning 40.

Framing the decade around what responds to effort, rather than what's fixed,
tends to be a more useful lens than treating every change as inevitable.

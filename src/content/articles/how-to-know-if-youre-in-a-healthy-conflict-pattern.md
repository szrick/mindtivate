Couples who never disagree aren't necessarily healthier than couples who
do — what predicts relationship trouble is less about whether conflict
happens and more about how it's handled.

## What research flags as risk factors

Criticism aimed at someone's character rather than their behavior,
stonewalling instead of engaging, and contempt (eye-rolling, mockery) are
more predictive of relationship strain than the frequency of disagreements
themselves.

Disagreeing well, including some conflict, tends to be a healthier pattern
than avoiding it altogether.

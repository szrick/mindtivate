If you've spent any time in r/xxfitness, you've seen this thread before:
someone asks "what bra do you actually lift in?" and the comments fill up
fast — because the "high support" label on most sports bras assumes running,
not bracing under a bar or kipping through a pull-up.

## The actual complaint, in the community's own words

Across a handful of recent threads, the same two failure points came up
repeatedly:

1. **Underwire or rigid front panels digging in during bench and overhead
   work**, when the ribcage expands on a big breath.
2. **Racerback bands riding up** during pull-ups and burpees, needing a
   mid-set adjustment.

That's a fit and construction problem, not a "you need more support"
problem — which is why generic "best sports bras" roundup articles weren't
actually answering the question.

## What to look for instead

- **Wide, non-rolling underband** that sits below the ribcage, not
  compressing it.
- **Wire-free molded cups** — structure from the fabric and seams, not a
  rigid wire that can't move with a deep breath.
- **A racerback or adjustable strap pattern** rated for pulling/overhead
  motion specifically, not just running.

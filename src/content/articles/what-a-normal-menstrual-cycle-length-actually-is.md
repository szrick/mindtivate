The 28-day cycle gets treated as the default, but it's an average, not a
target. Clinical guidance generally considers cycles anywhere from 21 to 35
days as within normal range for adults.

## What's actually worth tracking

Consistency matters more than hitting an exact number. A gradual or sudden
change in your own typical length and flow is a more useful signal to bring
to a doctor than comparing yourself to a textbook 28 days.

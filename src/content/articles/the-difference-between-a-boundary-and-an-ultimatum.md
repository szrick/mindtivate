A boundary describes what you'll do; an ultimatum demands what someone
else must do. Confusing the two is one of the more common reasons a
boundary conversation turns into a fight instead of a resolution.

## What that looks like in practice

"I'm not going to keep discussing this while you're raising your voice" is a
boundary — it's about your own action. "You need to stop raising your voice"
is a demand about someone else's behavior, which they can refuse.

Boundaries you can actually enforce are ones described in terms of your own
choices, not someone else's compliance.

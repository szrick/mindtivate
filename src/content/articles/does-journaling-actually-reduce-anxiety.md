Journaling shows up in almost every anxiety-management list, but not all
journaling works the same way.

## The style that has the most support

Expressive writing — describing a stressor and the emotions around it, not
just logging events — has the most consistent research support for reducing
anxious rumination.

Open-ended "what happened today" journaling has weaker evidence behind it.
If journaling hasn't helped before, the format may be the reason.

Decision fatigue — the sense that even small choices feel harder later
in the day — is a well-documented pattern, and it responds better to
reducing the number of decisions than to trying to push through it.

## What reduces the load

Pre-deciding routine choices (what to wear, what to eat) ahead of time, and
batching similar decisions together instead of spreading them through the
day, both reduce the number of times willpower gets tapped.

It's less about having more willpower and more about spending less of it on
choices that don't need to be made in the moment.

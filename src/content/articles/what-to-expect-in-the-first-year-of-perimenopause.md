The first year of perimenopause tends to be the most confusing, because
symptoms are often mild and inconsistent enough to have another explanation.

## Common early signs

Cycle length becoming less predictable, sleep disruption without an obvious
cause, and mood shifts that feel disproportionate to what's happening are
common early signals — often years before hot flashes, the symptom most
associated with menopause, appear.

Tracking cycle changes over a few months is one of the more useful early
signals to bring to a doctor if perimenopause is a possibility worth
discussing.

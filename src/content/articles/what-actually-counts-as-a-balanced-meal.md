"Eat a balanced meal" is common advice that rarely comes with a
definition. A practical, research-informed version is simpler than it
sounds.

## A plate-based starting point

- **Protein**: a palm-sized portion, roughly 20–35g depending on your needs.
- **Fiber-rich carbohydrates**: vegetables, fruit, whole grains, or legumes.
- **Fat**: a thumb-sized portion from whole-food sources.

This isn't a rigid rule — it's a starting template that tends to leave people
satisfied and steady on energy, which is usually the actual goal behind
"balanced" in the first place.

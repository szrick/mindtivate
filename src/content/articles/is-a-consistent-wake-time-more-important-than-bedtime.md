Most sleep advice focuses on going to bed at a consistent hour, but
circadian rhythm research points to wake time as the stronger anchor for
the body's internal clock.

## Why wake time anchors the cycle

Morning light exposure, which is tied to wake time, is one of the primary
signals that sets the timing of the next night's sleep drive. A stable wake
time, even after a late night, helps keep that signal consistent — which is
part of why sleeping in on weekends can make Monday mornings harder.
